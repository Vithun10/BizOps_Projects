# BizOps Projects

Business Operations portfolio projects by **Vithun TR**. Each project starts from a business decision, builds a synthetic dataset, analyses it with SQL, and ends in a recommendation backed by a model and a dashboard.

> **Synthetic data disclosure:** All companies, datasets and figures in this repository are fictional and generated by scripts. Every result is a simulated or modelled finding, not an outcome achieved for a real employer.

## Projects

| Project | Business question | Tools | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FLOW**: Customer Onboarding Throughput Diagnostic & Decision Model | Where does onboarding work wait, and which intervention best restores capacity? | MySQL, Excel, Power BI | [Flow/](Flow/) |
| **TRIAGE**: Risk-Based Client Onboarding & KYC Controls | Which applicants get heavier checks, and where should the risk threshold sit? | MySQL, Python, Power BI | [Triage/](Triage/) |

The two projects ask different questions. FLOW looks at process capacity: where work waits and what fixes it. TRIAGE looks at the risk threshold: who gets scrutiny, and what that costs in speed and missed risk.

## FLOW: headline findings (synthetic data)
- 2,711 applications across a 7-stage onboarding process, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026.
- 49.2% attainment against a 35-day SLA, with a 40.8-day mean and a 70-day P90 cycle time.
- Backlog grew from 203 to 360.
- Finance Approval ranks as the top bottleneck on a multi-factor measure (queue time, backlog growth and rework), not on average duration alone.
- Rework affects about 20% of eligible applications.
- In the base case no option reaches the 240/month target. Process redesign plus targeted staffing comes closest, with a gap of 17 cases per month.

## TRIAGE: headline findings (synthetic data)
- 2,000 synthetic applicants, with deterministic Low / Medium / High risk tiers. PEP and sanctions matches go to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).
- Threshold choice: 50 was chosen over the F1-optimal 40. That cuts EDD referrals from 32.2% to 15.2% of applicants, at the cost of recall (32% vs 55%).
- Average completion time is 4.0, 17.8 and 57.2 minutes for Low, Medium and High tiers.
- Overall drop-off is 21.85%, concentrated at document upload.
- About two-thirds of risky applicants are missed at the chosen threshold. This is stated openly as a trade-off.
- Segments too small to report reliably are flagged in the dashboard instead of being shown as findings.

## How to read a project
1. Start with the project `README.md` for the question, method and findings.
2. Read `assumptions/` for KPI definitions, scope and data-generation rules.
3. Check `sql/README_run_order.md` for the order in which the scripts run.
4. Review `powerbi/screenshots/` for the dashboard pages.
5. Read `limitations.md` before relying on any number.

## Notes
- **Power BI files:** the `.pbix` files are kept locally and are not in this repository. Dashboard screenshots and the DAX documentation are included, and the KPI CSVs in `data/processed/` are the inputs the reports use.
- **Reproducibility:** each project's data comes from a seeded generator script, so the datasets can be regenerated.
- **Modelled outcomes:** savings, capacity gains and risk trade-offs are scenario results. Nothing here claims realised business impact.
